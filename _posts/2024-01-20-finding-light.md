One of the most important aspects of photography is understanding light. Whether you're shooting landscapes, portraits, or street photography, light can make or break your image.

## Golden Hour

The golden hour - that magical time just after sunrise or before sunset - offers some of the most beautiful, warm light for photography. The low angle of the sun creates long shadows and a soft, diffused quality that's incredibly flattering.

## Blue Hour

Don't pack up your camera when the sun goes down! The blue hour, occurring just before sunrise or after sunset, provides a beautiful cool-toned light that's perfect for cityscapes and moody shots.

## Tips for Working with Natural Light

1. **Scout your location** - Visit ahead of time to understand how light moves through the space
2. **Be patient** - Sometimes waiting just a few minutes can transform a scene
3. **Embrace overcast days** - Clouds act as a natural diffuser, perfect for portraits

Remember, there's no such thing as bad light - just light that's better suited for different subjects.
